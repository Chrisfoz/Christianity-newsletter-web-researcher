# World Christianity News: Embracing Ethics in AI and Building a Faithful Future

In the ever-evolving landscape of technology, the careful integration of AI into society has become a focal point not only for governments and tech companies but also for religious institutions worldwide. Recently, two significant Christian leaders have stepped forward with messages that underline the importance of ethics, human dignity, and faith in this rapidly developing field.

## Elder Gerrit W. Gong Advocates for a Moral AI Future

Elder Gerrit W. Gong, a member of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, emphasized the necessity of a moral compass in AI systems during the AI Summit on Ethics and Artificial Intelligence held in Athens, Greece. His address highlighted both the potential benefits of AI and the significant challenges it poses. Elder Gong envisions AI as a tool that can foster personal growth, dignity, and ethical development if imbued with moral principles rooted in faith traditions ([Deseret News](https://www.deseret.com/faith/2026/05/26/gerrit-gong-athens-ai-religion-latter-day-day/)).

Gong's practical approach called for the intentional programming of AI to reflect human values, warned of existing religious biases in AI systems, and introduced the Consortium for Evaluating Faith and Ethics in AI to address these concerns. His message harmoniously aligned with recent declarations from the Vatican, advocating for a collaborative approach to navigate AI's integration into societal frameworks.

## Pope Leo XIV’s Groundbreaking Encyclical on Artificial Intelligence

In a historical move, Pope Leo XIV released his first encyclical, "Magnifica Humanitas," which directly addresses the implications of AI on society. The encyclical, which explores themes of ethics, human dignity, and technological power, serves as a moral framework for the digital age and calls for the "disarmament" of AI from military and economic dominance ([USCCB](https://www.usccb.org/news/2026/first-encyclical-pope-leo-urges-world-disarm-ai-amid-increased-reliance/)).

Pope Leo's document pushes for AI to be inclusive, transparent, and accountable, framing the conversation within the Catholic Church's longstanding tradition of social teaching, reminiscent of the transformative encyclical "Rerum Novarum" by Pope Leo XIII. It underscores the importance of applying ethical guidelines before AI becomes deeply entrenched in global systems, emphasizing dialogue and moral leadership in steering AI’s ethical development ([ChurchTechToday](https://churchtechtoday.com/the-pope-just-issued-the-most-significant-church-statement-on-technology-in-history/)).

## A Common Ground for Global Faith on AI

Both Elder Gong’s and Pope Leo’s teachings converge on essential points: that AI should serve humanity, not undermine it by concentrating power and perpetuating biases. This sentiment was echoed by various religious and secular leaders at the recent summits, highlighting the need for an ethical framework that respects both human and divine values as pivotal in the development of AI ([Christianity Today](https://www.christianitytoday.com/2026/05/christians-listen-pope-leo-encyclical-artificial-intelligence/)).

Moreover, these discussions have catalyzed the formation of faith-based coalitions aiming for a comprehensive ethical approach to AI that involves inputs from a diverse array of cultures and traditions. The rising interfaith dialogue on AI ethics signifies a hopeful movement toward a globally equitable digital future.

## Looking Ahead: Building Communities of Hope

As religious leaders continue to engage with technological advancements, the emphasis on safeguarding human dignity remains at the forefront. Moving forward, initiatives that integrate ethical considerations with technological innovation promise not only the betterment of individual lives but also the cultivation of communities ready to face the challenges of tomorrow's world.

The integration of faith and technology is not merely a conversation about limits but about possibilities—leveraging the power of AI to inspire, connect, and transcend barriers, always with an eye toward building a future grounded in love and compassion. As these discussions evolve, the ongoing commitment to ethical AI serves as a testament to the enduring values of faith, reinforcing the essential truth that even in an age of machines, humanity holds the greatest potential.
